 1. Critical Functional Missing Features
   * Pincode Serviceability Check: There is no way for a user to verify if
     their location is serviceable or to see an estimated delivery date
     before adding to the cart.
   * Related Products / "You May Also Like": The page lacks a recommendation
     section, which is essential for cross-selling and helping users find
     alternatives if the current item is low on stock.
   * WhatsApp / Bulk Inquiry Button: In the wholesale (B2B) context, buyers
     often have questions about large orders or custom requirements. A
     direct "Inquire on WhatsApp" button is standard for Indian wholesale
     platforms.
   . UX & Conversion Enhancements
   * Social Sharing: Resellers often need to share product links with their
     own customers or partners via WhatsApp/Social Media.
   * Sticky "Add to Cart" (Mobile): On mobile devices, the "Add to Cart"
     button disappears as the user scrolls through the description. A sticky
     footer button would improve the conversion rate.
   * Breadcrumb Depth: The breadcrumbs only show one category level.
     Supporting sub-categories or adding a "Back to [Category]" link would
     improve navigation.
   * Color Variants: If the product belongs to a series with different
     colors, there is currently no visual way to switch between them or see
     them as "related colors."
